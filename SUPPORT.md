## 1. Screen Recording & Microphone Setup

- **macOS 13+:** Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen & System Audio Recording** and enable **NidoBox**. Enable **Microphone** and **Speech Recognition** if you use voiceover audio or offline `.srt` subtitle generation.
- **Windows 10/11:** Capture Studio uses built-in Windows capture and offline `Windows.Media.Ocr`. Install additional OCR language packs under **Windows Settings → Time & language → Language & region**.

## 2. NidoDrop LAN Troubleshooting

- Connect your Mac/PC and phone to the **same local Wi-Fi subnet** (avoid isolated Guest Wi-Fi networks).
- Allow NidoBox through macOS Firewall or Windows Defender Firewall (Private Networks) when prompted.
- If VPN or virtual adapters are active, select your physical Wi-Fi/Ethernet IPv4 address in the NidoDrop adapter selector.

## 3. NidoShelf (macOS Top Notch & Floating Window)

- Move the pointer or drag files to the top-center notch / menu bar edge to expand NidoShelf.
- Click the detach icon in the shelf header to switch between **Top Notch Mode** and **Floating Window Mode**.

## 4. Resetting Data or Uninstalling

- **macOS:** Open **NidoBox → Settings → General** to clear Clipboard History or reset preferences. Move `NidoBox.app` to Trash to uninstall.
- **Windows 10/11:** Uninstall from **Windows Settings → Apps → Installed apps → NidoBox**.
