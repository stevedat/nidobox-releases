Privacy policy

NidoBox is engineered around local-first execution. Your screenshots, screen recordings, OCR text, clipboard history, archives, media files, and LAN transfers are processed directly on your device and are never uploaded to our servers.

Effective date: November 18, 2026 · Last updated: November 18, 2026 · Data Controller: NIDO HOLDINGS NEXT GENERATION COMPANY LIMITED (support@nido-holdings.com)

1. Scope and controller

This Privacy Policy explains how NIDO HOLDINGS NEXT GENERATION COMPANY LIMITED ("Nido Holdings", "we", "us", or "our") handles information in connection with the NidoBox software application across all distribution channels:

  • Apple Mac App Store Edition (Bundle ID: com.nido.box.mas)
  • Microsoft Store Edition for Windows 10/11 (App ID: com.nido.box)
  • Direct Desktop Installer Editions for macOS (.dmg, Bundle ID: com.nido.box), Windows (.exe / .msi), and Linux (.deb)

2. On-device processing (data that never leaves your device)

All core NidoBox features run locally on your hardware using native operating system frameworks:

  • Capture Studio, Screen Recording & Subtitles: Screenshots, window captures, video recordings, microphone/system audio streams, and generated .srt speech transcriptions are processed in local memory using Apple ScreenCaptureKit, AVFoundation, Speech, or Windows capture APIs. Media is saved only to local folders or the clipboard as chosen by you.
  • Offline OCR & Smart Redact: Text recognition and credential detection (emails, phone numbers, IP addresses, API keys) run strictly offline via Apple Vision on macOS and Windows.Media.Ocr on Windows.
  • NidoShelf & Clipboard History: Staged files and clipboard entries are stored exclusively in your local user container or memory. Password manager concealed markers (such as org.nspasteboard.ConcealedType) are respected, and you can clear history or disable clipboard monitoring at any time in Settings.
  • NidoImage, Media Converter & NidoArchive: Format conversion, EXIF metadata removal, archive inspection, extraction, and compression run entirely on your local CPU/GPU.
  • External Drive Monitor & Large File Scanner: Volume capacity metadata and file size listings are read locally on your device. File names, directory trees, and file contents are never transmitted externally.

3. NidoDrop local network transfer

When you explicitly start NidoDrop LAN, NidoBox launches a temporary HTTP server bound strictly to your local Wi-Fi or Ethernet interface (for example, 192.168.x.x or 10.x.x.x) protected by an ephemeral session token and PIN.

  • Files and two-way LAN clipboard text travel directly peer-to-peer between devices on the same local network.
  • No cloud relay, external STUN/TURN server, or remote storage is used. Stopping the station immediately closes the local socket and invalidates session tokens.

4. Network activity by distribution channel

Edition Automatic update / telemetry requests How updates are delivered
Apple Mac App Store (com.nido.box.mas) None. Self-updater and remote campaign manifests are disabled at compile time (MAS_BUILD). Managed exclusively by the Apple Mac App Store.
Microsoft Store (Windows 10/11 Store build) None when packaged for Store-managed updates. Managed by Microsoft Store (or user-initiated release check on standalone Win32 builds).
Direct Installers (.dmg, .exe, .deb) Checks latest-mac.json / latest-win.json / latest-linux.json via Cloudflare Edge / GitHub Releases unless turned off in Settings. Downloads signed release packages from GitHub Releases only when initiated or enabled by the user.

Zero-PII update check design (Direct Editions only)

When Direct Desktop Editions check for software updates via our Cloudflare Edge endpoint (nidobox-telemetry), the service resolves the right installer for your CPU architecture (such as Apple Silicon arm64 vs. Intel x86_64, or Windows ARM64 vs. x64) and computes aggregate version health counts using a rotating daily and monthly HMAC-SHA256 digest. Raw IP addresses, hardware serial numbers, MAC addresses, device names, and persistent cross-month identifiers are never stored. Aggregated operational counters automatically expire within 90 days. You can disable automatic update checks at any time in NidoBox Settings.

5. Voluntary user feedback and support requests

NidoBox never sends diagnostics or bug reports in the background. If you voluntarily open the Send Feedback dialog and submit a report, or email us directly:

  • Submitted fields: Feedback category, optional topic/subject, the message text you write, optional reply email address (only if you choose to enter one), and non-identifying environment metadata (app version, OS version, CPU architecture, UI language) if you keep diagnostics attached.
  • Purpose & storage: Stored in Cloudflare D1 solely to diagnose issues, reply to your inquiry (when an email is provided), and improve software reliability.
  • Retention & deletion: Feedback records are retained only as long as needed to resolve the reported issue and are permanently deleted within 7 business days upon request to support@nido-holdings.com.

7. Data sharing and third parties

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share any personal information with data brokers, advertisers, or analytics companies. Infrastructure providers involved only when you download Direct updates or submit voluntary feedback are:

  • Apple Inc. / Microsoft Corporation: Process Store purchases, downloads, and OS-level updates under their respective platform privacy policies when you install NidoBox from the Mac App Store or Microsoft Store.
  • GitHub, Inc. & Cloudflare, Inc.: Serve signed release manifests/installers (Direct editions) and host the voluntary feedback endpoint under standard TLS encryption.

8. Children's privacy

NidoBox is a general-purpose desktop utility rated 4+ (Apple App Store) / IARC 3+ (Microsoft Store) and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). If a parent or guardian believes a child has voluntarily submitted an email address via the support form, contact support@nido-holdings.com and we will delete it promptly.

9. Your privacy rights (GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, Vietnam PDPD)

Depending on your jurisdiction (including the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, California, and Vietnam under Decree 13/2023/ND-CP and Personal Data Protection regulations), you have the right to:

  • Delete local data immediately: Clear Clipboard History, empty NidoShelf, or reset all local preferences directly inside NidoBox Settings (or uninstall the app to remove its local container).
  • Access, rectify, or erase voluntary support data: Email support@nido-holdings.com to request a copy or permanent deletion of any feedback submission associated with your email address.
  • Opt out of Direct update checks: Toggle off automatic update checks in Settings on Direct Desktop builds.

Mọi tính năng cốt lõi của NidoBox đều vận hành cục bộ bằng tập lệnh gốc của hệ điều hành:

  • Nhận diện chữ OCR & Che thông tin nhạy cảm (Smart Redact): Chạy 100% ngoại tuyến bằng Apple Vision (macOS) và Windows.Media.Ocr (Windows).

  • NidoImage, Chuyển đổi Media & NidoArchive: Toàn bộ quá trình nén, đổi định dạng, gỡ siêu dữ liệu EXIF và giải nén/đóng gói tệp diễn ra hoàn toàn trên CPU/GPU của máy bạn.
  • Quản lý ổ đĩa ngoài & Quét tệp lớn: Thông tin dung lượng ổ đĩa và kích thước tệp chỉ được đọc cục bộ; tên tệp, đường dẫn và nội dung tệp không bao giờ bị gửi ra ngoài.

3. Truyền tệp mạng nội bộ NidoDrop LAN

Khi bạn chủ động bật NidoDrop LAN, ứng dụng mở một máy chủ HTTP tạm thời gắn trực tiếp vào card mạng Wi-Fi hoặc Ethernet nội bộ (ví dụ 192.168.x.x) kèm mã khoá phiên và mã PIN.

  • Tệp tin và Clipboard hai chiều đi trực tiếp ngang hàng (peer-to-peer) giữa các thiết bị trong cùng mạng nội bộ của bạn.
  • Không sử dụng máy chủ trung gian đám mây. Ngay khi bấm dừng trạm, cổng kết nối và mã phiên lập tức bị huỷ.
